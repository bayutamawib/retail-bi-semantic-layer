
# Executive Sales Dashboard

## Audience

CEO
Head of Commercial
Regional Managers

## Business Purpose

Provide a consistent view of commercial performance.

## Decisions Supported

1. Are sales increasing or declining?
2. Which countries drive revenue?
3. Which product categories perform best?
4. Where are margins declining?
5. Which products require commercial attention?

## Core KPIs

- Revenue
- Orders
- AOV
- Units Sold
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %

## Visualizations

1. KPI cards
2. Monthly revenue trend
3. Revenue by country
4. Revenue by category
5. Top products
6. Gross margin by category

## Reporting Cadence

Daily

## Data Source

BigQuery

## Semantic Layer

LookML
